# City Admin Flow (Deep Planning)

Goal: maintain city-level tutor quality, operational efficiency, and local revenue growth.

------------------------------------------------------------
1) FLOW A: TUTOR APPLICATION TRIAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- New tutor applications submitted in city scope.

Steps:
1. Queue sorting
   - by age, priority, subject demand, risk flags
2. Document verification
   - KYC, qualification, address, background checks
3. Decisioning
   - approve / reject / request more info
4. Audit trail
   - reason logs and operator identity

Decision points:
- High-demand subject applicant -> fast-lane verification
- Risk flag present -> mandatory manual review

Success output:
- Reliable tutor supply with controlled quality.

------------------------------------------------------------
2) FLOW B: CITY DEMAND-SUPPLY OPTIMIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Weekly city planning cycle.

Steps:
1. Demand analysis
   - subject demand hotspots and time-slot demand spikes
2. Supply analysis
   - available tutors by locality and subject
3. Gap identification
   - shortage zones and over-supply segments
4. Action planning
   - targeted tutor recruitment or incentive campaigns

Success output:
- Better match rates and lower student wait times.

------------------------------------------------------------
3) FLOW C: COMPLAINTS + DISPUTE RESOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Complaint raised by student/parent/tutor in city.

Steps:
1. Ticket intake and categorization
   - quality, behavior, payment, scheduling
2. Assignment
   - owner and SLA deadline allocation
3. Investigation
   - evidence review, session logs, chat history
4. Resolution
   - refund, replacement tutor, warning, suspension
5. Closure feedback
   - resolution quality score

Decision points:
- Severe misconduct -> immediate temporary suspension
- Repeated quality complaints -> tutor re-evaluation path

Success output:
- Faster issue closure and trust retention.

------------------------------------------------------------
4) FLOW D: CITY REVENUE AND PERFORMANCE GOVERNANCE
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Monthly operations review.

Steps:
1. Revenue and booking dashboard review
2. Funnel review
   - signup -> search -> booking -> repeat
3. Team performance review
   - verification SLA, dispute SLA, activation rates
4. Corrective action creation
   - process fixes and local campaigns

Success output:
- Sustainable city growth with better operational discipline.

------------------------------------------------------------
5) FLOW E: POLICY ENFORCEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- New global policy updates or local compliance incidents.

Steps:
1. Policy dissemination to city team
2. Process checklist updates
3. Spot audits on approved tutors and transactions
4. Exception reporting to global admin

Success output:
- Lower compliance risk and predictable governance quality.

------------------------------------------------------------
6) CITY ADMIN KPIs
------------------------------------------------------------

Primary:
- Tutor verification turnaround time
- City tutor activation rate
- Complaint resolution SLA adherence
- City booking growth %

Secondary:
- Repeat complaint rate
- Approval rejection quality (accuracy proxy)
- Local conversion improvements after interventions
