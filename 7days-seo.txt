# 7-Day SEO Execution Plan (RankJee)

Goal: move from setup to measurable indexing and first qualified organic leads.
Rule: prioritize revenue pages first (home tutor city pages, mock-test city pages, course-city pages).

---

## Day 1 - Search Console + Sitemap + Baseline

- Verify `rankjee.com` in Google Search Console (Domain property via DNS TXT).
- Submit sitemap: `https://rankjee.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Confirm live:
  - `https://rankjee.com/robots.txt`
  - `https://rankjee.com/sitemap.xml`
  - `https://rankjee.com/surat` (should resolve to tutor city landing)
- Request indexing for first 20 priority URLs from `seoplan/seo_publish_queue.csv`.
- Save baseline metrics:
  - impressions
  - clicks
  - CTR
  - average position

Deliverable:
- Search Console verified + sitemap submitted + baseline note.

---

## Day 2 - Rich Results Validation Sprint

- Open `seoplan/rich_results_validation.txt`.
- Validate template URLs in Google Rich Results Test:
  - Tutor city page
  - Tutor city-subject page
  - Mock test page
  - Mock test city page
  - Course city page
- Log PASS/FAIL and issue details in `seoplan/rich_results_validation.txt`.
- Fix any critical schema errors before publishing more URLs.

Deliverable:
- Validation sheet updated with current status for all template types.

---

## Day 3 - Quality Gate First Batch (20 URLs)

- Open `seoplan/seo_publish_queue.csv` and filter `status=needs-content`.
- For the first 20 URLs, enforce `seoplan/programmatic_quality_checklist.txt`:
  - unique title
  - unique meta description
  - one H1
  - unique intro
  - FAQ (3+)
  - visible CTA
  - internal links
  - canonical
  - HTTP 200
- Mark qualified rows as `ready`, then publish and mark `live`.

Deliverable:
- 20 high-quality URLs live.

---

## Day 4 - Internal Linking + Cannibalization Check

- Audit live SEO pages against `seoplan/keyword_url_map.txt`.
- Ensure one primary keyword maps to one canonical URL.
- Add/confirm related internal links on live pages:
  - city tutor -> mock city + relevant courses
  - mock city -> related tutor city + exam hub
  - course city -> tutor city + mock pages
- Fix any orphan or duplicate-intent pages.

Deliverable:
- Internal link graph strengthened for first live set.

---

## Day 5 - Publish Batch 2 (Next 20 URLs)

- Repeat quality gate workflow for next 20 URLs from queue.
- Publish only pages passing checklist.
- Request indexing in Search Console for newly live URLs.
- Re-check top 5 money pages manually for content quality and CTA clarity.

Deliverable:
- Additional 20 URLs live and submitted for indexing.

---

## Day 6 - Measurement + CTR Optimization

- In Search Console, check:
  - indexed vs excluded trend
  - soft-404 or duplicate warnings
  - low-CTR, high-impression URLs
- Improve title/meta for top 5 low-CTR pages.
- Verify no accidental `noindex` on money pages.
- Prepare weekly snapshot:
  - total live URLs
  - total submitted URLs
  - indexing trend
  - early clicks/leads

Deliverable:
- First optimization cycle completed on real data.

---

## Day 7 - Ops System Lock-In

- Run weekly review using template from `seoplan/live_ops_runbook.txt`.
- Finalize next 2-week queue:
  - top tutor city-subject pages
  - top mock city pages
  - top course-city pages
- Define recurring cadence:
  - 20 pages/week quality-passed
  - 1 weekly SEO review
  - 1 monthly technical audit
- Update `seoplan/seo_action.txt` checkboxes based on real completion.

Deliverable:
- Repeatable SEO operating rhythm in place.

---

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not publish thin or duplicate pages.
- Every page must have unique metadata + unique intro + FAQ + CTA + internal links.
- Publish fewer pages with high quality instead of bulk low-quality pages.
- Revenue-first order:
  1) Tutor city/subject pages
  2) Mock-test city pages
  3) Course-city pages
