# Global Admin Flow (Deep Planning)

Goal: optimize platform-wide revenue, quality, compliance, and scalable operations.

------------------------------------------------------------
1) FLOW A: PLATFORM COMMAND REVIEW (DAILY)
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Daily executive review window.

Steps:
1. Unified KPI snapshot
   - GMV, net revenue, active users, booking volume
2. Health checks
   - payment success, cancellation spikes, support backlog
3. Incident detection
   - anomaly alerts and red-flag segments
4. Priority assignment
   - operations, product, risk, growth owners

Success output:
- Platform risks identified early and addressed quickly.

------------------------------------------------------------
2) FLOW B: REVENUE FUNNEL OPTIMIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Weekly growth and monetization review.

Steps:
1. Funnel breakdown
   - visitor -> signup -> profile complete -> booking -> repeat
2. Segment analysis
   - by city, subject, role, acquisition source
3. Drop-off diagnosis
   - identify highest leakage stages
4. Intervention planning
   - pricing tests, onboarding changes, recommendation tuning
5. Impact tracking
   - monitor pre/post intervention metrics

Success output:
- Higher conversion and better LTV.

------------------------------------------------------------
3) FLOW C: POLICY + RISK GOVERNANCE
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Fraud alert, abuse report, compliance event, or audit cycle.

Steps:
1. Risk queue review
   - suspicious payment/tutor/account behavior
2. Severity scoring
   - low/medium/high/critical
3. Action routing
   - suspend, investigate, monitor, legal/compliance escalation
4. Preventive policy update
   - update rules, thresholds, and city admin guidance

Success output:
- Reduced platform abuse and financial risk exposure.

------------------------------------------------------------
4) FLOW D: CITY ADMIN PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Monthly city governance review.

Steps:
1. City scorecard comparison
   - growth, SLA adherence, quality index, dispute rates
2. Underperformer diagnostics
   - root-cause by process and staffing
3. Enablement plan
   - playbooks, training, operating standards
4. Accountability checkpoints
   - milestone-based review and escalation path

Success output:
- Consistent standards across all cities.

------------------------------------------------------------
5) FLOW E: PLATFORM CONFIGURATION CONTROL
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Strategic pricing/policy/product configuration updates.

Steps:
1. Configure commission and fee rules
2. Configure coupon and campaign guardrails
3. Configure category/subject taxonomy changes
4. Communicate changes to downstream stakeholders
5. Monitor impact and rollback criteria

Decision points:
- If negative impact exceeds threshold -> rollback workflow
- If region-specific outcome differs -> local override strategy

Success output:
- Controlled experimentation and safe scaling.

------------------------------------------------------------
6) FLOW F: EXECUTIVE REPORTING
------------------------------------------------------------

Trigger:
- Monthly/quarterly business review.

Steps:
1. Financial summary
   - revenue, take rate, cost centers
2. Growth summary
   - acquisition, retention, cohort movements
3. Quality summary
   - NPS, dispute trends, tutor quality indicators
4. Strategic action list
   - next quarter priorities

Success output:
- Data-backed strategic decisions for platform expansion.

------------------------------------------------------------
7) GLOBAL ADMIN KPIs
------------------------------------------------------------

Primary:
- Net revenue growth %
- LTV/CAC ratio
- Repeat booking cohort retention
- Platform take rate stability

Secondary:
- Policy violation incidence
- Payment failure trend
- City performance variance index
