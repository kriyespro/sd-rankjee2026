## 1. Product direction (no confusion)

| Layer | What it is | Role |
|-------|------------|------|
| **Primary GTM (now)** | **Home tutor marketplace** — find / list verified tutors | Main story on **public home page**; drives trust and local demand |
| **Core platform (existing)** | **Online exam / JEE prep** — MCQs, skills, learning, wallet | **Upsell** to the same families and tutors: “also practice & track progress on RankJee” |
| **Who can upsell** | Parents, students, **and** home tutors | Tutors refer students to tests; students upgrade to premium exam features |

**One-line pitch:** RankJee helps you **find the right home tutor**, and **level up with online exams** when you are ready.

---

## 2. Completed work (snapshot — do not re-scope)

These are **done**; new work builds on top.

- **Stack:** Django 5, Jinja2, HTMX, Tailwind/Alpine (CDN), Celery+Redis, SQLite dev / Postgres prod.
- **Apps:** `core`, `users`, `dashboard`, `assessment`, `learning`, `payments` (subscriptions), Axes.
- **URLs:** Django admin `/sd/`, staff dashboard `/admin/`, core site `/`.
- **Loops shipped:** Custom user, signup/login, MCQ assessment, weak-area → learning, retest,
  earnings tasks, skill paths, referrals, gamification, Razorpay subscription path (where enabled).

---

## 3. Roadmap — build order (what to do next)

Numbers are **sequence**, not calendar months. Finish earlier phases before starting later ones
unless a task is explicitly parallel.

### Phase HT-3 — Marketplace money (parallel after HT-2 starts)

**Status:** ✅ **Complete**

**Goal:** Parents pay platform; tutors get paid (manual payout OK at first). **Detail:** `hometutor.txt` Phase D.

| Done | Item |
|:----:|------|
| ✅ | Separate app **`hometutor_payments`** (not subscription `payments`): `MarketplaceOrder`, `TutorLedgerEntry`, `TutorPayoutRequest`. |
| ✅ | Razorpay checkout + verify + **`payment.captured` webhook** (`/hometutor/payments/webhook/razorpay/`); idempotent; `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` required when `DEBUG=False`. |
| ✅ | Platform fee (`HOMETUTOR_PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT`) + tutor credit on success; **manual payout** in `/sd/` (mark PAID → ledger debit). |
| ✅ | Fee-due **reminders** for recurring months (Celery) — optional follow-up. |

**Exit:** Money flow auditable; tutor balance + payout records for ops.

**URLs:** Pay from **My demo requests** when engagement is **ACTIVE** → `/hometutor/payments/pay/<engagement_id>/`.

## 4. What we are *not* doing first (avoid confusion)

- **Not** two equal homepages (tutor vs exam). Exam is **upsell**, not co-equal hero.
- **Not** reusing subscription `payments` models for tutor monthly fees — separate app
  (`hometutor_payments` per `hometutor.txt`).
- **Not** nationwide launch with zero supply — pilot city first.
