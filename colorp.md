RankJEE — recommended palette — Built on three pillars:

Navy (#0D2B5E) as the foundation — signals authority, trust, and seriousness. Every top coaching brand (Allen, FIITJEE, Resonance) leans into dark blues because they communicate academic weight.
Saffron Orange (#F4711C) as the action color — culturally resonant in India, energetic, and pairs beautifully with navy for CTAs, countdown timers, and "Rank #1" moments.
Gold (#F5B731) for achievement — rank badges, topper medals, leaderboard crowns. This is the color students chase.
Emerald (#1CA97B) for correct answers, progress bars, and success states — adds freshness and avoids the dull red/green quiz cliché.
