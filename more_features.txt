Based on the Indian education landscape (Competitive exams like SSC/Banking/JEE, mobile-first usage, and "pocket money" motivation), here are some "common sense" features that would make **RankJee** a viral hit among Indian students:

### 1. 🏆 Daily Scholarship "Jackpot" Quiz
Instead of just random tests, host **one high-stakes quiz daily at a fixed time** (e.g., 9:00 PM). 
*   **The Hook:** Top 10 scorers get an instant ₹10-₹50 wallet credit. 
*   **Why:** It creates a "fear of missing out" (FOMO) and daily habitual usage.

### 2. 📲 One-Click WhatsApp/Telegram Referrals
In India, WhatsApp is the primary social network.
*   **The Feature:** A dedicated "Invite via WhatsApp" button with a pre-written viral message: *"Bhai, check this out! I just earned ₹50 by watching ads and taking a 5-min test. Join using my link: [URL]"*
*   **Why:** Makes sharing frictionless for the 500M+ Indian WhatsApp users.

### 3. 📊 "Accuracy vs. Speed" Analytics
Indian competitive exams (SSC/Banking) are all about speed.
*   **The Feature:** On the result page, don't just show the score. Show how much time they spent per question vs. the "Topper's Time."
*   **Why:** Students are obsessed with optimizing their time to finish 100 questions in 60 minutes.

### 4. 🗺️ State/District-Wise Leaderboards
Competing against "everyone" feels impossible. Competing against people in your city feels achievable.
*   **The Feature:** Let users select their State (e.g., Bihar, UP, Maharashtra) and show a **Local Leaderboard**.
*   **Why:** Enhances peer motivation and creates local "champions."

### 5. 📵 Data-Saver "Revision Bites" (Audio/Text)
Many students in rural areas have unstable 4G/5G connections.
*   **The Feature:** Allow users to download "Revision Bites"—short 1-minute audio clips or text summaries of lessons—so they can learn without watching high-bandwidth videos.
*   **Why:** Solves the "buffering" frustration and saves their daily 1.5GB data limit.

### 6. 🔥 7-Day "Tapasya" Streak
Gamify the consistency.
*   **The Feature:** If a user completes 1 test daily for 7 days, they get a "Tapasya" badge and a small XP/Credit bonus.
*   **Why:** Taps into the cultural value of disciplined study and keeps retention high.

### 7. 🗣️ Hinglish Support (Hindi + English)
Most Indian students study in "Hinglish."
*   **The Feature:** Provide an option to switch the interface text to Hindi or provide explanations for MCQ answers in both English and Hindi.
*   **Why:** Reduces the entry barrier for millions of State Board and Hindi-medium students.

---

**Which of these should we build first?** My recommendation would be the **WhatsApp Referral Hook** and the **Daily Jackpot Quiz** to drive immediate platform growth.
